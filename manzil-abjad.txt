Mashriqi Abjad Letter Assignments for 28 Manāzil
=================================================

The project uses the Mashriqi (Eastern) abjad ordering, following the
Shams al-Ma'arif (al-Buni) and the standard tradition of manzil-letter
correspondences. The key difference from the Maghrebi ordering is in
positions 15–21 and 26–28:

  Mashriqi: ...ن(14) س(15) ع ف ص(18) ق ر ش(21)...ض(26) ظ(27) غ(28)
  Maghrebi: ...ن(14) ص(15) ع ف ض(18) ق ر س(21)...ظ(26) غ(27) ش(28)

Rationale: Although the Maghrebi order is considered older as a general
abjad system, no classical source on manzil-letter magic uses it. The
Shams al-Ma'arif uses Mashriqi ordering despite al-Buni's North African
origin, and all modern practitioners follow suit.

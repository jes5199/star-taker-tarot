Mashriqī Abjad Letter Assignments for 28 Manāzil
=================================================

The project uses the Mashriqī (Eastern) abjad ordering, following the
Shams al-Maʿārif (al-Būnī) and the standard tradition of manzil-letter
correspondences. The key difference from the Maghribī ordering is in
positions 15–21 and 26–28:

  Mashriqī: ...ن(14) س(15) ع ف ص(18) ق ر ش(21)...ض(26) ظ(27) غ(28)
  Maghribī: ...ن(14) ص(15) ع ف ض(18) ق ر س(21)...ظ(26) غ(27) ش(28)

Rationale: Although the Maghribī order is considered older as a general
abjad system, no classical source on manzil-letter magic uses it. The
Shams al-Maʿārif uses Mashriqī ordering despite al-Būnī's North African
origin, and all modern practitioners follow suit.
